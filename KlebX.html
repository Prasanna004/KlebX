About KlebX

KlebX is an integrated machine learning and genomic profiling tool for phenotype prediction in Klebsiella pneumoniae. It combines machine-learning-based hvKp-like prediction with antimicrobial resistance profiling, virulence-associated markers, sequence typing, K-locus characterization, plasmid replicon detection, genome quality metrics and resistance-virulence co-occurrence analysis.
